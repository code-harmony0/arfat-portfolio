# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing static site (no build step): index.html, style.css, site.js, work/*/index.html. Deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Everyone who reaches the site: hiring managers and CTOs, recruiters, and fellow engineers. Most skim for under ten seconds and read little. They decide whether Arfat is a senior engineer worth a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio for Arfat Rozewale, a senior React Native engineer who wants to be hired into a role owning mobile products, with relocation open. Success is a skimmer leaving the first screen knowing what he has shipped, how he decides, what he owns, and how he solves problems, then emailing him.

## Positioning

The sole mobile engineer of a live Dubai marketplace (Zonesso) who owns architecture, payments, tests and every store release, and who can show real production problems with their causes and fixes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Role to sell: senior mobile engineer who owns products. Web (Next.js) is supporting proof.
- Team-size rule: solo on Zonesso and QPlease only; Keys On Rent, Eco Crew, Cone, JIFFL were two-person teams.
- JIFFL and QPlease are UK projects at Mantiqh, never "client work".
- Only CV or repo-verified numbers on the page. No em-dashes. No placeholders visible to visitors; open items live in CONTENT-NEEDED.md.

## Brand Commitments

Existing dark theme, vermilion accent, Geist and Geist Mono, real screens in real phones. Must not look AI-made (see memory: avoid-ai-design-look).

## Evidence on Hand

Zonesso repo (ARCHITECTURE.md, Fastfile, pipelines, tests, commit history), CV, live store listings. Absent: GitHub link, resume PDF, testimonials, latency numbers.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before claims: every sentence about skill points at work a visitor can check.
2. Skim first: the first screen must answer what he built, how he decides, what he owns, how he solves problems.
3. Stories are true: symptom, cause, fix, drawn from repos or his own words.
4. One action: email.
