The product

Zonesso is a consumer marketplace for buying and selling cars in Dubai. People browse and filter listings, post their own car, chat with or call sellers, save favourites and get notified about offers.

What I owned

I am the sole mobile engineer: architecture, payments, testing and every release, on iOS and Android. My scope has since expanded to the Next.js website and admin dashboard, with light NestJS changes.

What I built

  • Five payment rails: Stripe, Apple In-App Purchase, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay, N-Genius, with VAT.
  • Arabic and English with full right-to-left layout, about 580 translation keys.
  • The release pipeline: Gradle flavors, Fastlane lanes, and CI running Jest, Detox and Appium.
  • Real-time chat between buyers and sellers.

What was hard

  • Five payment methods behind one checkout, agreeing with the server.
  • Notification taps that arrived before the app finished launching.
  • A codebase grown to 170 dependencies and 53,900 lines.

How I solved it

  • Led the React Native 0.75 to 0.81 migration as an architecture cleanup: 39% less code, 57 packages removed.
  • Parallel start-up and a 100-entry LRU cache with tiered TTLs for faster cold starts.
  • Deep-link retry logic for the notification race.

What I shipped

The app on the App Store and Google Play, the CI and release pipeline behind it, and the website that sits beside it.
