Cone

The mobile app for Cone, a work-management product for accounting and professional services firms. Built at Mantiqh Technologies by a two-person team.

The product

Cone helps accounting and professional services firms run their work. This is its mobile app: clients and their accountants share tasks and documents, sign, pay and message, all in one place.

The brief

A private client product, built at Mantiqh Technologies by a two-person team. The app puts a client's tasks, documents, signatures, payments and conversations with their accountant in one place, on a phone.

The wider product

Cone is work management for accounting and professional services firms.

  • Proposals
  • Billing
  • Payments

What I built, and why it fits

  • Who you are: Auth0 authentication for secure access.
  • What you sign: document editing, e-signatures and PDF generation.
  • What you pay: payment processing.
  • What you ask: client-to-accountant messaging.

Those four map onto the trust chain of an accounting relationship. Each one has to be dependable before the next one matters.

What was hard

Financial workflows have to feel simple while keeping the trust that tax documents demand: secure exchange, clear task visibility and fast communication.
