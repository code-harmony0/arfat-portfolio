The product

Eco Crew makes recycling pickups easy: list what you want to sell, capture your items, choose a pickup slot, track the pickup and see your impact.

The brief

Eco Crew is a live app with over 50,000 downloads and a 4-star rating. In a two-person team, my part was the pickup experience and the weight of the install.

How I read it

A pickup app lives or dies on location: the right address, the right slot, a pickup you can follow. So I built the pickup and delivery flows around geolocation, with Google Maps for the place and Firebase behind it.

The size cut was a separate problem. On a consumer app, install size matters, so I worked it down by about 30%.

What I built

  • Geolocation-based pickup and delivery flows on React Native, Firebase and Google Maps.
  • Application size reduction of approximately 30%.

What I shipped

The app is live on the App Store and Google Play, with over 50,000 downloads and a 4-star rating. The pickup flows and the smaller app size are my work. Links above.
