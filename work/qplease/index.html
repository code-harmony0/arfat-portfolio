The product

QPlease replaces physical queues with a digital one. The app tells customers when it is their turn, and the business sees wait times and queue status in real time. Built for clinics, restaurants and service centres.

The brief

A UK queue and appointment platform wanted its customers to stop standing in line. They scan a QR code, choose a service and join a virtual queue.

My part

I was the only mobile developer, so the whole app surface was mine: QR scanning, service choice, joining a queue, live queue status, notifications and messaging, on iOS and Android.

How it works

  1. Scan

    A QR code at the business.

  2. Choose

    Pick a service, see the queue.

  3. Wait remotely

    Notifications say when it is your turn.

  4. Plans

    From a free trial up to Platinum.
