The product

JIFFL is a large app with two sides: an employer experience and a job seeker experience, each with its own flows, from onboarding to profiles, applications and messaging.

What I owned

  • A two-person mobile team, from first scoping to store release.
  • Stakeholder communication, and turning business requirements into technical execution plans, timelines and resourcing.

What was hard

Two user sides in one large app, employer and job seeker, each with its own flows, permissions and dashboards.

What I shipped

Stable, on-schedule releases to Google Play. Job-seeker engagement rose by approximately 30% and the app size fell by 15%.
